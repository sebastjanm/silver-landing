# Brand Voice & Copy Deck — NakupSrebra.com / Elementum

## Voice Guide

**How the brand talks**: We speak like a knowledgeable friend over coffee — calm, direct, honest. We name the reader's real worries without dancing around them. We use concrete images (money in a drawer, coins you can hold) not abstractions (value preservation strategies). Short sentences with rhythm. We're confident without being salesy; we've been through crises and we've seen what lasts. No hype, no hurry. Just truth told plainly.

### Chapter 1: Od kamna do kovanca / From Stone to Coin

**Slovenian**:
```
Headline: Od kamna do kovanca
Subheadline: Srebro je služilo kot denar že tisočletja. Od antičnih civilizacij do današnjih dni. Ni modna naložba. Je časovno preizkušena shramba vrednosti.
Timeline caption: Tisočletja kasneje srebro še vedno ohranja vrednost
```

**English**:
```
Headline: From Stone to Coin
Subheadline: Silver served as money for millennia. From ancient civilizations to today. It's not a trend. It's a time-tested store of value.
Timeline caption: Millennia later, silver still holds value
```

**Rationale**: Opens with a concrete historical fact (silver as ancient money) to establish credibility and position silver as timeless, not trendy. The headline evokes the journey of money itself.

**English**:
```
Headline: When Would You Sell?
Subheadline: When you need the money. No questions. No penalties. No waiting.

Three cards:
- When: You decide
- How much: You decide
- Why: No explanation needed
```

**Rationale**: Tackles the "what if I need my money back" worry head-on. Three-beat rhythm (no questions, no penalties, no waiting) is memorable. Control language (you decide) reassures.

### Chapter 6: Kako začnete / How You Start

**Slovenian**:
```
Headline: Kako začnete

Step 1: Brezplačen pogovor
15 minut. Brez pritiska. Brez prodaje. Spoznajte me, povejte mi o svojih ciljih, vprašajte vse, kar vas zanima.

Step 2: Prilagojen predlog
Na podlagi pogovora pripravim predlog: koliko, zakaj, kako. Transparentno. Brez skritih stroškov. Vi odločite, ali želite nadaljevati.

Step 3: Varno lastništvo
Ko se odločite, uredimo nakup. Kovine gredo v švicarski trezor na vaše ime. Dobite certifikat. Lahko preverite stanje kadarkoli.
```

**English**:
```
Headline: How You Start

Step 1: A free conversation
15 minutes. No pressure. No selling. Get to know me, tell me about your goals, ask anything.

Step 2: A tailored proposal
Based on our conversation, I'll prepare a proposal: how much, why, how. Transparent. No hidden costs. You decide whether to proceed.

Step 3: Secure ownership
When you decide, we arrange the purchase. The metals go into a Swiss vault in your name. You get a certificate. You can check the balance anytime.
```

**Rationale**: Three-step path is clear and calm. "Spoznajte me" (get to know me) signals personal relationship, not corporate transaction. Each step ends with reader control.

## Voice Notes

**What changed from the original**:
1. Removed AI-tell patterns (no "unlock", "discover the power", "journey", "seamless")
2. Varied sentence length (short punches: "Quietly. Unnoticed." / longer explanatory: "Based on our conversation...")
3. Concrete over abstract (drawer, hands, screen vs. "value preservation mechanisms")
4. Named worries directly (scam, lock-in, need money back) and answered them
5. Personal voice (Spoznajte me / Get to know me) not corporate
6. No hype, no urgency tactics — calm confidence
7. Both languages written natively for their readers, not translations

**What stayed the same**:
- Regulatory compliance (past results disclaimer, no absolute claims)
- All figures and sources (LBMA, HICP, dates)
- Meaning and structure
- Trust without invented details
