## Verification Process (CRITICAL)

**Before using ANY image:**

1. **Download the image** to `/workspace/public/images/`
2. **Open and VIEW the image file** (use image viewer or browser)
3. **Verify it shows:**
   - ✅ Actual physical SILVER (not gold, not copper, not crypto tokens)
   - ✅ No Bitcoin symbols (₿)
   - ✅ No cryptocurrency logos or references
   - ✅ No US dollars, Euro notes, or other paper currency
   - ✅ No digital trading screens or apps
4. **If verification passes:** Use the image and document in this file
5. **If verification fails:** DELETE the image immediately, do not use
